1. Executive Summary & Business Context

Bellcourt Health Administrators, Inc. is a third-party administrator (TPA) headquartered in Nashville, TN, administering healthcare benefits for 38 self-funded employers (453,330 covered lives) and delegated utilization management (UM) for Riverbend Health Plan (153,700 Medicare Advantage & Marketplace members). In FY2025, Bellcourt generated $112.2M in total revenue ($112.4M forecast in FY2026), with $76.0M derived from self-funded per-employee-per-month (PEPM) administrative fees.

In FY2026, Bellcourt entered an acute existential crisis. Net operating profit margin collapsed from 14.0% in FY2023 down to 8.0%, driven by $1.9M in service credit penalties paid out to Riverbend for turnaround delays under CMS-0057-F. Riverbend placed Bellcourt on a formal Corrective Action Plan (CAP) in July 2026 and threatened an open contract re-bid in October. Concurrently, Bellcourt’s largest self-funded employer, Harlan Freight Lines ($8.06M annual PEPM revenue, 44,100 lives), is evaluating competitor TPAs due to opaque reporting and clumsy denial letters.

5. The Intake Clock Trap & CMS-0057-F Penalties

The primary operational flaw causing the $1.9M penalty is an architectural disconnect between federal regulations and PACE:

The Statutory vs. PACE Clock Disconnect

Under CMS-0057-F (effective Jan 2026), Medicare Advantage standard decision turnaround is 7 calendar days (168 hours) from receipt.

• Day 0 (Receipt): Fax lands in inbox ➔ Legal Clock Starts
• Day 3.2 (Keying): Case keyed into PACE ➔ PACE Clock Starts
• Day 7.0 (Decision): PACE reports 3.8 days ("Met"), Audit shows 7.0+ days ("Breached")!

PACE dashboards reported ~98% timeliness, blinding executive leadership while actual performance dropped to ~90%. Riverbend assessed 4% quarterly fee penalties per percentage point below 97%, totaling $1.9M.

The Incompleteness Drag (32.3% of Faxes)

Across 640 faxes/day, 32.3% arrive incomplete (missing clinical notes, CPT codes, or member IDs).

  • Deficiency notices take 2 to 3 days to issue manually.
  • In `pa_requests_2025_2026.csv`, turnaround time strictly adds: $\text{Turnaround} = \text{Intake} + \mathbf{Pend} + \text{Review}$.
  • The statutory clock keeps ticking during pends; prolonged pends guarantee SLA failure.
  • Clinics call repeatedly to check receipt, driving 2,400 daily calls and 20% abandonment.

8. Option A: Ingestion, OCR & Adversarial Defense

The Intake & Triage Engine (`src/intake_engine.py`) intercepts faxes, webhooks, and phone submissions at the perimeter, executing validation and security sanitization before cases enter clinician queues:

1. Automated 14-Field OCR Pipeline

Extracts all 14 statutory fields required by PACE from raw TIFF/PNG images via Tesseract OCR and morphological table analysis:

  • Patient Demographics: Name, DOB, Gender, Address
  • Subscriber Identification: Member ID, Employer Client Code
  • Provider Information: Requesting Physician, NPI, Tax ID
  • Service Details: Facility Name, Place of Service
  • Clinical Codes: Primary/Secondary ICD-10, CPT/HCPCS
  • Clinical Documentation: Notes summary & attachment verification
  • Statutory Timestamp: Received timestamp (`received_ts`)

2. Eligibility Matching with Fallback

Cross-references `member_eligibility_extract.csv`. If an OCR typo degrades the Member ID, the engine executes a secondary fuzzy match on Last Name + Date of Birth, eliminating wasted coordinator phone tag.

3. Dual-Layer Adversarial Guardrail

Provider documents are untrusted external inputs. The guardrail (`src/llm_guardrail.py`) neutralizes malicious manipulation:

Layer 1: Deterministic Regex Filter

Zero-latency scanning for injection patterns (`"ignore previous instructions"`, `"system override"`, `"developer mode"`, script injections). Instantly neutralizes malicious tokens.

Layer 2: Semantic LLM Security Judge

OpenRouter (`openai/gpt-4o-mini`) semantic classifier detects deceptive social engineering (e.g. "Pre-approved verbally by Medical Director") or manufactured clinical urgency.

4. Automated Deficiency Management

For the 32.3% of faxes arriving incomplete, the engine identifies missing items in 60s and transmits an automated fax-back Pend Notice, freezing the statutory turnaround clock.

10. Option B: Policy Hierarchy & Evidence RAG

The Clinical Copilot (`src/clinical_copilot.py`) eliminates the 14.2-minute policy lookup sinkhole by enforcing Bellcourt's official Hierarchy of Authority (`GOV-01`) before invoking criteria verification:

Deterministic Policy Hierarchy Routing Engine (`src/policy_resolver.py`)

Vector embeddings fail in healthcare because `MP-101 v1`, `MP-101 v2`, and `UM-MEMO-2025-19` share 95% word similarity. Our engine uses deterministic logic:

• Tier 1: Federal & Addendum Overrides: If Riverbend MA, Medicare NCD/LCD rules take precedence (e.g. `RIVERBEND-ADDENDUM §2.1` CGM covered for insulin treatment across all DOS).
• Tier 2: Employer Summary Plan Description (SPD): Checks contract exclusions (Harlan bariatric exclusion `§6.4`; Bright COE `§5.3`; PT annual visit caps).
• Tier 3: Active Medical Policy by Date of Service (DOS): Pinned by DOS (DOS < 2026-01-01 ➔ v1; DOS ≥ 2026-01-01 ➔ v2).
• Tier 4: Invalidation of Informal Memos: Automatically rejects `UM-MEMO-2025-19` as void under `GOV-01 §1.4`.
