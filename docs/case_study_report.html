1. Executive Summary & Strategic Context

Bellcourt Health Administrators is a Tennessee-based Third-Party Administrator (TPA) managing healthcare benefits for 38 self-funded employers (453,330 covered lives) and delegated Prior Authorization (PA) for Riverbend Health Plan (153,700 Medicare Advantage & Marketplace members). In FY2025, Bellcourt generated $112.2M in revenue (~$112.4M forecast in FY2026), with $76.0M derived from self-funded administration fees (PEPM).

In 2026, Bellcourt entered an acute operational and financial crisis. Operating margins eroded from 14.0% to a forecast 8.0%, driven by $1.9M in service credit penalties paid to Riverbend. Riverbend issued a formal Corrective Action Plan (CAP) in July 2026 and threatened an open contract re-bid in October. Simultaneously, Bellcourt’s largest commercial client—Harlan Freight Lines ($8.06M annual PEPM revenue, 44,100 lives)—is actively evaluating competitor TPAs due to opaque reporting and clumsy denial correspondence.

3. Data-Backed Diagnosis: The Dual Vulnerability

A rigorous audit of 2,400 historical cases (`pa_requests_2025_2026.csv`), weekly staffing logs (`nurse_staffing_weekly.csv`), and 120 QA-audited cases (`qa_audit_sample_2026.csv`) disproves the "understaffing" narrative and identifies two structural root causes:

Vulnerability 1: The Intake Clock Trap

Under CMS-0057-F (effective Jan 2026), Medicare Advantage standard decision turnaround is cut from 14 days to 7 calendar days (168 hours) from receipt.

The Legacy Blind Spot:
1. Fax lands in shared inbox: Legal Clock Starts
2. Sits 3.2 days in backlog before Marcus Bell keys it.
3. Intake keys case: PACE Clock Starts (3 days late!)
4. PACE reports 98% on-time; Riverbend audit reveals 90% actual timeliness.

Furthermore, 32.3% of faxes arrive incomplete. Because deficiency notices take 2-3 days to issue manually, cases sit in `PEND_FOR_INFO` status while the statutory clock continues to accumulate, guaranteeing SLA failure.

7. In-Depth: Option A Intake & Triage Engine

The Intake & Triage Engine (`src/intake_engine.py`) transforms Bellcourt’s chaotic front door from a 3-day manual bottleneck into an instant, validated pipeline.

1. Multi-Channel Ingestion & 14-Field OCR

The system processes faxes via Tesseract OCR and table parsing, extracting all 14 statutory fields required by PACE:

  • Patient Name, DOB, Gender, Phone, Address
  • Subscriber ID / Member ID, Client Code
  • Requesting Provider Name, NPI, Tax ID
  • Facility Name, Place of Service
  • ICD-10 Diagnosis Codes & Descriptions
  • CPT / HCPCS Service Codes & Requested Procedure
  • Clinical Summary & Attached Progress Notes
  • Statutory Receipt Timestamp (`received_ts`)

2. Automated Eligibility Verification

Matches extracted Member ID against `member_eligibility_extract.csv`. If OCR misreads an ID character, it executes a fuzzy lookup on Last Name + DOB, preventing intake coordinators from spending 20 minutes calling clinics for basic typos.
