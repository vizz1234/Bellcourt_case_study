Vulnerability 1: The Statutory Clock Trap & Fax Drag

The Legal vs. System Disconnect

Under CMS-0057-F (effective 2026), Medicare Advantage standard decision deadline is 7 calendar days (168 hours) from receipt.

Legacy Timeline:
• Day 0: Fax lands in folder ➔ Legal Clock Starts
• Day 3: Intake keys into PACE ➔ PACE Clock Starts
• Day 7: Case completed ➔ PACE says "4 days (Met)", Audit says "7+ days (Breached)!"

The Incompleteness Drag (32.3%)

Across 640 faxes/day, 32.3% arrive incomplete (missing clinical notes, CPT codes, or member IDs).

  • Deficiency notices take 2–3 days to issue manually.
  • Cases sit in `PEND_FOR_INFO` while statutory turnaround continues to burn.
  • Call Center Impact: 2,400 daily calls, 57% are clinics asking "Did you get my fax?" (20% abandonment).

Compliance & Risk Regulatory Guardrails

Bulletproof Regulatory Compliance Architecture

1. Zero Auto-Denials

Under CMS rules and state law, AI cannot deny care. Approvals route to Nurse Approvers for 1-click sign-off. All proposed adverse decisions route strictly to Physician Reviewers.

2. Arizona MD Licensing

Effective July 1, 2026, Riverbend MA denials for Arizona members require an Arizona-licensed MD. The Copilot automatically routes these cases exclusively to Bellcourt's 2 Arizona-licensed MDs.

3. Texas AI Disclosure

Texas law (effective Jan 1, 2026) requires written patient disclosure of AI usage. The Copilot automatically appends the statutory AI disclosure statement to all Texas determination letters.

CEO Protection: Dana Whitfield's fear of "becoming the next AI headline" is completely neutralized. The system operates as a clinical evidence locator and policy router with mandatory human clinician sign-off.
